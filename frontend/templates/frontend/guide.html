One skeleton

Every page ends up in layout/base.html: the HTML head, the styles, the script and the slots for the header, the main part and the footer.

A few layouts

Public pages, signed-in visitors, sign-in pages and the staff area each have a layout. On top of the public one there are page layouts: a home page with a full-screen picture, a banner, a plain band, a listing, a detail page, an article like this one, a legal page and a centered message.

A page picks a layout and fills in its words. Everything else comes from the layout.

Changing things

  • Colours, fonts and sizes: the variables at the top of layout.css.
  • Site name and menus: LAYOUT in the settings.
  • One page only: empty or fill a block in that page.
